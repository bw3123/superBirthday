[Verse 1]
We met beneath a rain-washed sky,
You smiled, and something changed in me.
The drops kept falling, but somehow
My little world began to grow.
You turned a gray day into hope,
A quiet peace I didn’t know.

[Pre-Chorus]
You bring your clever, curious mind,
Your wild ideas, your generous heart.
And every dream we trade together
Feels like a brand-new place to start.

[Chorus]
Happy birthday, Baran, may the whole world celebrate you,
For the joy you bring, the thoughtful things you do.
May each wish you carry find its way into the blue,
You deserve the happiness that keeps on finding you.
Happy birthday, Baran, I’m so glad you’re here with me,
You make life brighter than I ever thought it could be.

[Verse 2]
Movie nights and laughing loud,

My favorite dream, my closest friend.
So smart, so nerdy, full of wonder,
With a smile that softens every day.

[Pre-Chorus]
We draw tomorrow side by side,
And make our little plans come true.
I’m grateful for each page ahead
As long as I can share it with you.

[Chorus]
Happy birthday, Baran, may the whole world celebrate you,
For the joy you bring, the thoughtful things you do.
May each wish you carry find its way into the blue,

Happy birthday, Baran, I’m so glad you’re here with me,

[Bridge]
That rainy day feels close enough
To touch whenever skies turn gray.
You gave me hope, you gave me peace,
And made a home inside the day.
So here’s to every dream we share,
And all the years still coming through.
The world is lucky, Baran—
I’m luckier to celebrate you.

[Final Chorus]
Happy birthday, Baran, may the whole world celebrate you,
For your giant heart and all the good you do.
May your brightest dreams come true, one by one, in time,
May happiness surround you in a hundred little signs.
Happy birthday, Baran, I’m so glad you’re here with me,

[Outro]
Happy birthday, Baran,
Here’s to every dream with you.
